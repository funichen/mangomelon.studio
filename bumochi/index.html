Privacy by default

Designed for no accounts, no ads, and no third-party ad tracking. Routine data is built to stay on the device unless you choose to share or contact us.

💤

Empathy Break

After 5 minutes of screen time, your pet says "I'm tired" — and the app politely cools down for 5. No doom-scroll.

🎨

No ad pressure

No "watch this ad to keep your streak" interruptions. Any future purchases should be optional, parent-visible, and separate from a child's daily progress.

🌸

Calm by design

Gentle palette, no flashing animations, no casino-style reward loops. It should feel like a calm family tool, not a slot machine.
