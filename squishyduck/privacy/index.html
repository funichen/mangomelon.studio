SquishyDuck — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2025

1. Who We Are

MangoMelon Studio LLC ("we," "us," or "our") is a small independent studio that creates playful, peaceful, and creative digital experiences. SquishyDuck is designed to help users reduce stress through simple, calming interactions.

2. What We Collect

We do not collect or store any personal information.

We do not track user activity across apps or websites.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or data profiling tools.

If you choose to donate via in-app purchase, all payment processing is handled securely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details, name, or any sensitive information.

3. In-App Purchases

Our app may include optional in-app donations handled through Apple's App Store. All transactions are processed by Apple and subject to Apple's privacy policies.

We do not access or store your purchase information.

4. Children's Privacy

SquishyDuck is designed for a general audience and is safe for children.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 13.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store personal data, there is no information to access, edit, or delete. If you contact us directly, your email will be used only for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, feel free to reach out:

support@mangomelon.app
